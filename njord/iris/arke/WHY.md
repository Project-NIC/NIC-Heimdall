# Arke — why

Arke's own entries; the board's graveyard is Iris's (`../WHY.md`).

## LTO 6S

Lithium titanate 6S, 10,8–16,8 V, charging deep in frost: no 6S LTO BMS with a stated standby was
found, and a smart one's draw could be the whole budget. Left for sodium-ion 4S charged as LiFePO4,
whose BMS and MPPT are the common LiFePO4 ones.

## A fifth sodium cell

5S to use more of the cell: to 3,8 V a cell it is 19 V, over the `TPS629206`'s 18 V absolute maximum on
the station's 12 V node, and held to 17 V it gains less than 4S's wider window.

## The U535 on Arke

The U5's Stop at 2,2 µA against the H523's 150–270 µA saves ~1–2 mAh a day at 13 V, against a day of
4–35 Wh. The H523 kept.
