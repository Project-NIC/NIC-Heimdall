# Sakura — the graveyard

Rejected alternatives and superseded states, with the reason. Nothing returns from here and
nothing is deleted from it.

**The board and the build are Ceres's, and so is their graveyard** — the line-front exception,
the read methods, the 200 MHz timer, the covers and the enclosures before the dish, the dish's
back, the supply, the values of that board alone: `../WHY.md`. What is here is Sakura's own.

## The NTC on the lead — superseded by the digital part

Sakura carried an NTC on the lead for its own compensation and reported no temperature. **The
disease models want the canopy temperature beside the wetness**: the `TMP117`/`STS35` on the board
reports the plate's on `0x0001`, as a bought unit of the class does.

## The comb under PTFE, the FR4 leaf, a radiation shield — superseded

**PTFE is hydrophobic and beads the dew the plate exists to see**; resistive leaf sensors are
painted hydrophilic for that. Soldermask chalks in the sun in a season or three. A radiation shield
keeps the plate wet past noon and copies nothing of a leaf. Now borosilicate to the sky at 45°, no
shield.

## A solid plate over the glass against hail — rejected

**It stops the rain, which is wetness, and the sky, which cools the plate to dew.** A coarse
stainless mesh, ≥ 60 % open, stops hail that would break 1 mm of glass and leaves most of both.

## A full copy of Ceres's `HARDWARE.md` and `FIRMWARE.md` — superseded

**Two copies of one board drift at the first change**; both are difference files, as
`../../tesla/pip/` is to Tesla's board.

## A stainless tab epoxied into the channel — superseded

The glass-backed build's holder sat in the channel, **in the one seal**. It is glued onto the
disc's outer face: the glass carries the seal, the holder only the load.

## PETG or ASA named for the printed back — superseded by the condition

The printed back must let no moisture through to the fill, so its plastic is specified by
glass-class water uptake and vapour permeability, not by name; **PETG and ASA take 0,1–0,3 %** and
were named before the condition.
