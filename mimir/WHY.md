# Mimir — the graveyard

Rejected alternatives and superseded states, with the reason. Nothing returns from here and
nothing is deleted from it.

## The mini-station — a suggestion of three sections, superseded

The board began in `core/MINI-STATION.md` as Kronos, one card and Palatine, the Mayak a fourth
section or a board of its own, unnamed. It is now a named board of the station: the Mayak a
section, an Argus on the Bifrost's port 2, two NodBus ports out instead of three.

## The Argus on a trunk of the Mayak — wrong, corrected

**A Mayak trunk is a MasterNOD link to a card on Kronos's time bus; an Argus is off that bus** and
takes its clock from its spur. So the Argus sits on the Bifrost's port 2, as a remote Argus sits on
a spur, and the Mayak's second trunk is the spare for a Bifrost outside.

## One board with Proteus — rejected

Proteus is the head, the clock and a Bifrost; Mimir adds an Argus and Palatine. **One PCB with
sections unfitted by use would be drawn wrong for both.**

## Hermes on a power body — superseded by a section

A card on the Mayak's power body was the station's form; **on one PCB the card is a section** — its
I²C, `ALERT`, `ID` and 3,3 V traces — and the body is not fitted.

## Counted against five boards — corrected

The document weighed Mimir against "five boards", which were six with Hermes and were never the
point: **Mimir is one PCB carrying the station's classic structure**, the time bus as M-LVDS pairs
across it, and it is weighed against a full Heimdall. Polaris is bought and plugs into `TIME IN 1`;
the backup is the Mayak's own, unchanged.
